Scheduled inflows & outflows

Amounts are signed from the account's point of view: a paycheck into checking is +, rent is −. Detected items (source detected) are excluded from the projection until confirmed. Every 2 weeks and twice a month are kept apart on purpose (both get called "biweekly"): the first drifts through the calendar and lands three times in some months, the second sticks to two fixed days. Pays links an outflow to the card, loan or utility it pays, so that account's own due-date event isn't counted a second time; a suggested link is a guess from the name until you save it.
