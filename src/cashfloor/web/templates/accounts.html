Accounts

Give each account a short key (used in config and archive folder names), a type, and tick pool for the accounts you actually spend from. Last 4 is how downloaded statements get matched to an account (SimpleFIN doesn't supply it). For cards/loans/utilities you can also set the next due date by hand if no statement has been parsed yet. For a loan or mortgage, Min / payment is the monthly installment — the only figure of theirs that enters the projection (a greyed value is what sync saw in the loan's own payment history). Pay day is the day of month a card with no known due date is assumed to be paid in full (see Settings → assume card payoff); a greyed number is what sync learned from the card's own payment history, and anything you type overrides it.
